The valve rules: before you take money out

  1. Is it unexpected?
  2. Is it necessary?
  3. Is it urgent?

Three yeses: open the valve. A sale that ends tonight is not an emergency. Refill after every use.

How it works: time to each level = (level target minus what you already have) divided by your weekly amount. It ignores interest, so a savings account that pays some interest gets you there a little faster. Keep the tank in a separate account, a little out of sight. Education only, not financial advice.
