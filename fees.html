How much does a 1% fund fee really cost you?

A yearly fee comes out every year, whether the fund goes up or down. Compare a low-cost index fund with a fund that charges 1%. The numbers filled in below are the example from our video: $10,000 for 30 years at 7% a year.

How it works: each year the fund grows by the example rate minus the fee, and monthly additions go in at the start of each year. Real returns change every year and can be negative. Past growth does not promise future growth. Only invest money you will not need for at least five years, and build your safety fund first. Education only, not financial advice.
