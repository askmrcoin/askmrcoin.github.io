      Your next 12 months

      How it works: your monthly budget is two paychecks, never the yearly average (pay × 26 ÷ 12), because ten months a year you only get two. Bills due on days 1 to 14 come from paycheck 1, days 15 to 31 from paycheck 2. Bills bigger than half a paycheck are split half and half, so the money is set aside before the due date. Paydays repeat every 14 days from the date you enter. Education only, not financial advice.
